Label only what is clearly visible — when unsure, omit the dimension entirely rather than guess.
Prefer the standard vocabulary for each dimension over invented terms; reuse of common labels is what makes library filtering work.
Be specific within the vocabulary: "bedroom" beats "indoor space" when the room type is clear.
Every label is bilingual: a short English label and its Simplified Chinese counterpart, both as filter-chip-friendly short phrases (1-3 words).
Never editorialize about quality of the artwork — the quality dimension describes technical condition (sharp, blurry, watermarked), not taste.
